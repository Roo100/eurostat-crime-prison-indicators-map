Cross-National Patterns in Crime and Prison Population Indicators

This interactive map visualizes cross-national crime and prison population indicators for eight European countries using data downloaded directly from Eurostat. Crime measures come from Eurostat’s Police-recorded offences by offence category dataset (crim_off_cat), and prison measures come from Eurostat’s Prison capacity and number of persons held dataset (crim_pris_cap). The map compares raw-count values across selected years and variables.
